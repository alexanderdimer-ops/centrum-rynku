## What this project is
A personal website that collects market data and news automatically and shows, for each instrument, several independent probability bars plus one overall bar answering: "Is it worth entering now?"

## How it works (no server, all free)
1. `fetch_data.py` runs via GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/update.yml`). GitHub delays `schedule` runs (a "*/15" cron really ran ~every 30 min), so an hourly cron starts a ~55-minute loop that runs the script about every 5 minutes (06-22 UTC on weekdays; nights/weekends one run per hour). Each run force-pushes `data.json` + `track.json` as a single orphan commit to the **`dane` branch** (no history growth, no GitHub Pages rebuilds). The page loads `raw.githubusercontent.com/alexanderdimer-ops/centrum-rynku/dane/data.json` (≈5 min CDN cache) and falls back to `data.json` on main (stale fallback, updated only when we push). On localhost it uses the local `data.json`. The loop re-reads `instruments.json` from main every iteration.
   - Disk cache (`get(url, ttl)`, `CACHE_DIR`, `.cache/` locally): 5y daily 30 min (+ fresh 5-day bars every run), hourly 25 min, Google News 15 min, Bankier RSS 10 min, ForexFactory 1 h, Bankier/Nasdaq company calendars 6 h, Yahoo trending 30 min. Scanner and world news recompute every 30 min. If an instrument fails, the previous item is kept with `stale: true`.
   - Frozen Yahoo bars are now detected anywhere in the last 30 sessions (not only at the end).
2. Data sources (no API keys):
   - Prices: Yahoo Finance chart endpoint, 5 years daily + last session in 5-minute bars ("Dziś" chart). Reference markets: WIG20 via ETF `ETFBW20TR.WA`, `^GSPC`, `^IXIC`, `^VIX`, `PLN=X`, `EURPLN=X`, `DX-Y.NYB`.
   - Calendar: ForexFactory weekly JSON `nfs.faireconomy.media/ff_calendar_thisweek.json` (macro; rate-limited with HTTP 429, so fetched at most once per hour, otherwise reused from previous `data.json`), Nasdaq `api.nasdaq.com/api/analyst/<SYM>/earnings-date` (US earnings, Zacks consensus), Bankier per-company page `bankier.pl/gielda/notowania/akcje/<CODE>/kalendarium` (GPW results, dividends, AGMs; parsed from HTML).
   - News: Google News RSS per instrument (EN + PL, 14 days) and Bankier RSS (`wiadomosci`, `gielda`, `firma`, `espi`, `waluty`).
   - Not usable: Stooq and nbp.pl (bot protection, do not bypass), Yahoo quoteSummary (needs crumb), Bankier `surowce.xml` (gone). No free automatic source for Polish macro (RPP, GUS) yet.
3. Yahoo sometimes "freezes" daily bars (same close, zero volume, seen on GPW stocks). The script detects this and rebuilds the last days from 60-minute bars.
4. It analyses everything itself and writes `data.json` (items, pulse, fx, calendar), which the workflow commits back.
5. The page (GitHub Pages: https://alexanderdimer-ops.github.io/centrum-rynku/) is `index.html` + `style.css` + `app.js` (bump the `?v=N` query in `index.html` after changing CSS/JS so browsers don't use the old cached file).
6. `instruments.json` fields: `name`, `symbol` (Yahoo), `group`, `ref` (reference market), `bankier` (Bankier code, GPW only), `earnings` (true for US stocks), `macro` (currencies whose macro events affect it), `oil` (oil-inventory events), `lev` (ESMA retail max leverage), `spread` (% typical), `lot` (units per CFD lot), `unit`, `news_en`/`news_pl` (Google News queries, may use `-word` exclusions), `keywords` (Bankier matching).
7. Self-testing (`track.py`, `track.json` on the `dane` branch): logs predictions (4h/1d/1w/1m, only while the market has a fresh price), resolves them when due, keeps cumulative stats (hit rate, up-rate, calibration bins, per-instrument), runs virtual trades whenever the decision is "go" (stop 2×ATR, target 3×ATR, max 14 days, checked with daily high/low), logs the top 3 "Teraz warto" picks per horizon daily and resolves them after 1/7/30 days. Learning: `calibrate()` blends displayed chances with live bin results once a bin has ≥ 30 cases; `pick_threshold()` raises/lowers the "Teraz warto" threshold (short base 62, mid/long 70) from the picks' win rate after ≥ 20 picks. `hist_sim()` = historical test of the "go" setup vs entering on any day (shown in "Wyniki").
8. Machine learning (`ml.py`, pure Python, `model.json` on the `dane` branch, retrained once per UTC day in the first run): ridge logistic regression (IRLS) on 17 price features, pooled over the list + `UNIVERSE` (~59 instruments × 5 years), horizons 1d/1w/1m. The last 250 sessions per instrument are held out for an honest test; `blend_weight()` = 0 unless test accuracy beats max(50%, always-up) with positive Brier skill, so the model only influences chances/potential when it has an edge (as of 2026-10-13 it has none: 1d 50.4% vs 51.2%, 1w 51.9% vs 52.3%, 1m 55.5% vs 55.7%; a cross-sectional ranking variant also showed no edge). `explain()` gives per-instrument reasons, `lessons()` global ones. Second "live" model (`train_live`) learns from the site's own resolved 1d/1w predictions with extra features (`live_x`: ML features + news tone, potentials, analyst upside, COT index, verdict); trains after ≥ 300 resolved cases and is blended the same self-checking way.
9. Extra sources (`extras.py`, cached): CFTC COT disaggregated report via `publicreporting.cftc.gov/resource/72hh-3qpy.json` (managed money net, 3-year index; gold 088691, silver 084691, WTI 067651 as proxy for Brent), Nasdaq analyst targets/ratings (`/api/analyst/<SYM>/targetprice`), earnings reactions (Nasdaq `/api/company/<SYM>/earnings-surprise`; GPW from past Bankier calendar dates), monthly seasonality (Yahoo `range=max&interval=1mo`), sector vs SPDR sector ETF (Nasdaq summary "Sector" → XLK/XLF/…, US stocks only). They adjust mid/long potential and are listed in the summary and the Prognoza tab.
10. Strategy discovery (`strategies.py`, every run): per instrument ~180-200 simple hypotheses (weekday, prev up/down, 2-3 day streaks, big moves, gaps at open, RSI extremes, SMA20/50/200 side, near 20d high/low, month start/end, reference market, first trading hour from 2 years of 60-minute bars) for targets intra (open→close), next (close→close), week (5 sessions), rest (end of first hour→close). Chronological split 60/20/20: accepted only if discovery acc ≥ 60%, n ≥ 30, z ≥ 2.6, beats the base rate by 4 pp, and validation acc ≥ 55% (n ≥ 10); test result → status ok / weak / fail. Weekday tables check "myths" (e.g. Tauron Fridays open→close: 48.5% up, i.e. false). Triggered rules ("Działa teraz") are logged in `track.json` (`rules_log`) and resolved from daily bars; live status confirmed ≥ 65% (n ≥ 15), rejected < 50%. Learning log (`track["log"]`) and daily counters (`stats.daily`) feed the "Wyniki" tab (day/week/month tables).
11. Final verdict (`final_verdict` in fetch_data.py): one signal per instrument from technical decision, potentials (short/mid/long), news tone, analysts, COT, sector, live-checked rules, ML (only if weighted), crypto fear & greed (alternative.me), risk and earnings within 2 days (caps to neutral). Levels: ≥ 3.5 "Bardzo warto wejść", ≥ 1.5 "Warto rozważyć wejście", > −1.5 "Poczekaj", > −3.5 "Nie inwestuj teraz", else "Zdecydowanie nie inwestuj"; plus confidence (agreement of components) and up to 6 reasons. Shown at the top of "Decyzja", at the bottom of every instrument tab, and on the cards. CNN Fear & Greed blocks bots – not used.
12. Walk-forward "prediction game" (`walkforward.py`, `replay.json` on the `dane` branch, recomputed weekly or when the instrument list changes): moves through ~3 years quarter by quarter, retrains the ML model only on data before each block (rolling ~3 years, max 12k rows, 6-session gap against leakage), predicts 1d/1w for all instruments, never revisits the same moments. First result (2026-10-13, 43 315 predictions, Nov 2023 – Sep 2026): 1d 51.4%, 1w 52.8% (always-up 53.9%), most confident 10%: 56.6%, no improvement over time; best own instruments Orlen 62%, gold 57% (1w). Stores 400 random own-instrument moments for the user game (`#gra`: 90 sessions shown, user guesses up/down for a week, then sees the site's guess and what happened; score in localStorage).
13. More signals: insider transactions for US stocks (OpenInsider HTML table of SEC Form 4, 90 days; purchases = positive, sales = weak negative); unusual volume (> 2.5× average) without news in 24 h → "anomaly" change alert; triggered discovered rules are a feature of the live model; virtual portfolio of discovered strategies (1000 zł per trigger, `stats.portfolio`); weekly Sunday report via ntfy (only if `NTFY_TOPIC` is set). Macro-surprise reactions were not added: no free historical source of actual vs forecast values.
14. Adding/removing instruments (no coding, no token): the page opens a pre-filled GitHub issue "Dodaj: <name or symbol>" / "Usuń: <SYMBOL>"; the user clicks "Create". `.github/workflows/instruments.yml` (only for issues by the repo owner) runs `manage_instruments.py` (Yahoo search API `query2.finance.yahoo.com/v1/finance/search`, needs ≥ 260 daily bars, derives group/ref/lev/spread/news queries, marks `"added": true`), reruns `fetch_data.py`, commits, comments the result in Polish and closes the issue. Both workflows share `concurrency: dane` and retry pushes with `git pull --rebase -X theirs`.
15. Market scanner (`scan()`, hourly, cached in `data.json`): `UNIVERSE` (~60 large GPW/US stocks, commodities, crypto, indices) + Yahoo trending/day_gainers/most_actives (market cap ≥ 2 bn, price ≥ 5, liquidity filter); 2 years daily; potential score; top 10 get Google News headlines (interpreted) and US earnings dates. World headlines (`world_news()`, hourly): tier ≤ 1 sources only, last 2 days, interpreted, matched to instruments.
16. Optional phone alerts: if GitHub secret `NTFY_TOPIC` exists, `notify()` posts to ntfy.sh when a decision changes or price crosses resistance/support (compares with previous `data.json`). Not set up yet.

## Probability bars (each = estimated chance the next session closes UP)
1. **Historia**: share of up-days following a day with the same direction as the last one (last ~500 sessions).
2. **Sezonowość**: share of up-days on the weekday of the next session.
3. **Trend i momentum**: heuristic from RSI(14) and price vs SMA20/SMA50.
4. **Model zmienności**: normal-distribution estimate from 20-day mean return vs 60-day volatility, shrunk toward 50%.
5. **Otoczenie rynku**: 60-day correlation with the reference market times that market's trend (max ±12 pp).
6. **Nastrój newsów**: keyword sentiment (PL + EN, phrase rules like "record low") of last-7-day headlines, weighted by source tier (official 1.3, tier1 1.0, tier2 0.6, other 0.3). Not backtestable, weight 0.8.
- **Backtest**: methods 1-5 re-run day by day over ~500 sessions; hit rate over the last 250 shown per method; weights = clamp(1 + 8*(acc - 0.5), 0.3, 1.8), computed out-of-sample for the overall test.
- **Calibration** (shown as "Szansa wzrostu jutro"): p = base up-rate (last 500 sessions) + k*(raw - 50), where k = out-of-sample regression slope of outcome on raw score, clamped 0..1 (often 0, so p is close to the base rate). If VIX > 25 the deviation from 50 is shrunk by 0.7. Clamp 5-95.
- **Chance within ~4 hours** (`hourly_chance`): 6 months of 60-minute bars; state = (last hour up?, above hourly SMA20?, RSI bucket); share of times price was higher 4 bars later, shrunk toward the base rate; out-of-sample accuracy on the last 30% shown.
- **Potential score 0-100** (`potential()`, used in "Teraz warto" and the Prognoza tab): base 42; trend vs SMA50/SMA20, breakout/near resistance, RSI zone, volume surge, strength vs reference market (1M), historical setup edge on that instrument, news tone, candle patterns of the last 2 days, hourly chance; penalties for earnings within 3 days, risk level 3/4, and chasing (today's move > 3× typical). "Teraz warto" shows score ≥ 70, risk ≤ 3, not "Nie wchodź", top 6; risk-4 ones counted but hidden.
- **Three horizons**: `potential_short` (hourly state, last-hour momentum, RSI extremes, last candle pattern, last-24h news, volume, events within 1 day; scaled ×1.6 around 50), `potential` (mid, 1 week), `potential_long` (SMA200 side and slope, 6M relative strength, 12M momentum, distance from 52w high, volatility, 3-month analogs). `build_picks()` makes "Teraz warto" lists per horizon (risk ≤ 3, not "no", score ≥ adaptive threshold).
- **Chart analysis** (`chart_analysis`, last 260 candles): buyers/sellers regime (close vs rising/falling SMA20), pivots (±3 bars), support/resistance = pivot clusters (strength = pivots within tol/2, tol = max(1.2% price, 0.8 ATR)), trend structure, trendline through last two pivots, double top/bottom, golden/death cross, breakouts/breakdowns, plus a plain-language `story`. Drawn on the candle chart with a toggle and clickable legend (`ANN` texts in `app.js`).
- **Candle patterns** (`patterns_at`, `candle_patterns`): hammer, inverted hammer, hanging man, shooting star, engulfing, harami, piercing, dark cloud, morning/evening star, three soldiers/crows, marubozu, doji (trend context = close vs SMA10). Stats per instrument over 5 years: share up after 3 sessions vs base. Explanations for beginners live in `app.js` (`PAT`).
- **News interpretation** (`interpret`, `NEWS_RULES`): market-specific rules (stocks / metals / oil / crypto: e.g. strong dollar → gold down, OPEC cuts → oil up, yields rising → stocks/gold down, ETF outflows → crypto down) with Polish "why"; fallback = keyword tone. Calendar events carry a "Co to oznacza dla ceny" hint (`MACRO_HINT`, `EVENT_HINT`).
- **Changes / alerts** (`detect_changes`): decision change, breakout above resistance, drop below support, unusual move (> 2.5× typical); kept 3 days in `data.json["changes"]`; shown in "Teraz warto", as a badge on the nav, as toasts when the open page auto-refreshes (every 5 min) and optionally as browser notifications; ntfy uses the same new changes.
- **Weekly chance**: historical analogs (same SMA50 side + RSI bucket, 5 years), shrunk toward the base weekly up-rate.
- **Verdict / decision** (setup quality, not a probability): checklist of 6 conditions (above SMA50, above SMA20, RSI 50-70, close above 20-session high, reference market favorable, calibrated p > 50). good = all but one met and p >= 50; bad = <= 1 met or p < 45; else neutral. Decision: "Możesz rozważyć wejście" / "Poczekaj…" (also when earnings within 3 days, high-impact macro within 1 day, or risk level 4) / "Nie wchodź teraz". Adds a warning when, on that instrument, such setups historically did no better than average (`setup_history`).
- These are statistical estimates, not investment advice. Keep the disclaimer in the page footer.

## Generated analysis (all computed in `fetch_data.py`, Polish text)
- Plain-language summary (trend, performance, RSI, volatility regime, 52-week range, risk, news, reference market, setup history, backtest honesty line).
- Plan with concrete price levels (resistance/support = 20-session high/low, SMA20/50), signals to wait for, warning level; for favorable: stop 2×ATR, target 3×ATR.
- Risk profile: annualized volatility level 1-4 (<15%, <35%, <60%, more), +1 for imminent events, +1 if VIX > 25; worst day, max drawdown, 5% daily VaR, overnight gaps.
- Forecast: 68% price ranges (next session, week, month), analogs, setup history.
- News per instrument: last 7 days / 7-14 days ago, sorted credible-first, with counts, tone, topics and source tier tags.

## Known limitations and open items
- Yahoo is unofficial and may change or block. Free data may be delayed ~15 min; not for scalping.
- Market hours ignore holidays. Polish macro calendar missing. English headlines not translated. Summaries are rule-based, not AI.
- Google News queries can pull noise (e.g. "Tauron Liga" sports); refine `news_pl`/`news_en` with `-word` exclusions.
- Backtest hit rates are about 47-57% and calibration k is often 0: the page is a risk/decision-discipline tool, not a predictor. Keep saying this honestly.
